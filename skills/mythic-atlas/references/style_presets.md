# Style Presets Reference

Use one of these exact keys in the `style:` field of the intake YAML.

## punk_occult
Distressed black paper, electric magenta, cyan accents, antique cream microtype, torn labels,
occult chart wheels, graffiti marks. Underground concert poster meets esoteric infographic.
Best for: bold, rebellious, contemporary, counterculture subjects.

## wood_relief
Deep-carved walnut or oak plaque, visible grain, chisel cuts, pyrography, relief shadows.
Raised lettering, scroll banners, botanical medallions. Museum-quality artisan craft aesthetic.
Best for: heritage, family legacy, earthy subjects, gifts meant to last generations.

## woodburned_infographic
Joined planks, burned line art, shallow engraving, scorched edges, folk-art geometry.
Monochrome warmth with amber highlights. Handmade cabin-wall map energy.
Best for: outdoorsy, rustic, homesteader, naturalist subjects.

## sepia_heritage
Aged parchment, brass, lace, handwritten notes, archival stamps, recipe cards, heirloom objects.
Amber light, botanical tones. Victorian memorial collage.
Best for: genealogy, family history, memorial pieces, sentimental gifts.

## museum_esoteric
Black vellum, antique-gold rules, restrained magenta, diagrammatic panels.
Editorial serif, sacred geometry, scholarly exhibition energy.
Best for: philosophical, academic, ceremonial, high-end collector pieces.
